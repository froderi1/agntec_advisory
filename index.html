01 / DEFINE

Transformation Definition

What should change — and why?

Turn an ambition into a clear brief. Identify the gap between business needs and current capabilities, then align outcomes, engineering processes, scope and priorities before launching a project.

A direction you can act onTransformation brief, target capabilities and a prioritised roadmap.

02 / REVIEW

Independent Review

Does the proposed direction make sense?

Get an independent view of an assessment, strategy or proposal. Challenge the business case, assumptions, dependencies and trade-offs before the next major commitment — including whether the case for change holds at all.

A grounded basis for the next decisionAssessment of gaps and risks, with practical recommendations on the proposed direction.

03 / SELECT

Selection Advisory

Which platform and partners fit your needs?

Compare platforms and implementation partners against your business and engineering requirements. Structure the evaluation, examine proposals and challenge the trade-offs before you commit.

A defensible selection decisionEvaluation criteria, a structured comparison and a reasoned recommendation.

04 / ASSURE

Project Assurance

Is the project on track — and if not, why?

Readiness · Before launch
Test the business case, project setup and delivery plan before launch.
Independent Oversight · During delivery
A fixed monthly retainer with agreed time and cadence: health reviews and a monthly PM sparring session; gate reviews and an external-advisor seat on the steering committee as agreed.
Recovery · When delivery stalls
Diagnose why delivery has stalled and set out priorities to regain control.

A clear view of project healthEvidence-based findings, a risk heat map and prioritised actions.

I advise, diagnose and challenge, supporting the sponsor and project leadership. Project management and delivery ownership remain with your team and delivery partners; I do not take over either role.
